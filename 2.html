The weather is very important in our daily life. It changes from one season to another and affects our activities. In summer, the weather is usually hot and sunny. People enjoy going to the beach and spending time outdoors. In winter, the weather becomes cold and sometimes rainy. Many people prefer staying at home and drinking hot drinks. Knowing the weather forecast helps us choose the right clothes and plan our day better.
